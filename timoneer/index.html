About Timoneer Strategic Partners

In addition to our own capital, we utilize strong relationships with family offices, institutional investors, and private high net worth individuals to execute our transactions. This approach allows us the flexibility to pursue both control and meaningful minority investments.

Our team takes tremendous pride in being a value-added partner to our portfolio companies and management teams. We focus on investing behind opportunities where we can support our teams in driving transformational change through strengthening operational initiatives (system implementation, process automation, enhanced technology utilization) and tactical and accretive M&A.

In addition to our investment professionals, we leverage an impressive network of executives and operating resources spread throughout North America.

Our Story

Timoneer is co-led by Ben Frazier and Matt Joen, whose professional careers intersected in New York City over a decade ago. With a combined 20+ years of private equity, investment banking, and operating experience, Timoneer’s team delivers a differentiated, and owner & entrepreneur friendly, alternative to traditional private equity.

Our name, Timoneer (timonier, Fr.), was born out of our entrepreneurial, operationally focused, and hands-on approach to investing.

We leverage our relationships with accomplished Executives, Operating Partners, Board Advisors, and strategic LPs to provide the top-tier resources and support you would expect to receive from a larger organization — all while delivering the hands-on attention of a more tactical and nimble partner.

— Ben & Matt, Co-Founders of Timoneer

Investment Criteria

TARGET INDUSTRIES

  • Mission Critical Services, including:
    • Business, Commercial, Consumer, Environmental, Financial, Industrial, Infrastructure, Residential, Technology / IT
  • Healthcare (Services & Providers)
  • Niche Manufacturing and Distribution
  • Other sectors selectively

BUSINESS PROFILE

  • Entrepreneur or owner-operated
  • Differentiated service offering and sustainable value proposition
  • Defensible market or brand position
  • Favorable sector attributes in niche and fragmented industries
  • Value creation potential via organic growth initiatives and strategic M&A
  • Operating / optimization opportunities
  • HQ in the United States

FINANCIAL CRITERIA

  • Established businesses with a history of profitability
  • Strong recurring, predictable or contracted revenue streams
  • 10%+ organic growth rate
  • $1–$15 million of EBITDA (no criteria for add-ons)
  • Attractive margin profile and cash flow conversion dynamics

INVESTMENT TYPES & SIZE

  • Recapitalization (majority and significant minority), Buyout and Growth Equity selectively
  • Ability to invest up to $25 million of equity into an individual platform, and can leverage our LP network to size up, if appropriate
  • Transaction values up to $150M

Our Founders

Ben Frazier

Managing Partner · , Los Angeles, CA

ben.frazier@timoneersp.com

Ben is a Founding & Managing Partner at Timoneer Strategic Partners and is a member of the Timoneer Investment Committee.

Ben is primarily responsible for deal sourcing and qualification, conducting due diligence, valuation, transaction execution, operational improvements and portfolio monitoring.

Prior to Co-Founding TSP, he was an investment professional at Marlin Equity Partners and L Catterton, where he focused on identifying and executing investments across the enterprise software and consumer spaces, respectively, and supporting existing portfolio investments. Previously, Ben was an investment banker in the Consumer and Retail Group at Bank of America Merrill Lynch in New York, where he focused on leveraged buyouts, mergers and acquisitions, equity and debt financing activities, and other strategic advisory initiatives.

Ben graduated magna cum laude from the University of Southern California’s Marshall School of Business, where he earned a B.S. in Business Administration with a concentration in Finance.

Matt Joen
